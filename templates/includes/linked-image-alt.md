## Some linked images do not have alt text [WCAG 1.1.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/quickref/?versions=2.0#non-text-content) [WCAG 4.1.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/quickref/?versions=2.0#name-role-value)

The alt text of an image becomes the link text when the image is wrapped in a link. If there is no link text, it becomes an empty link. Empty links are announced to screen reader users and confuse them. They will not know if they are missing information.
